Morality Is Not Make-Believe

Non-Kantian morality

Many people think we need abstract rules to be good. Immanuel Kant is famous for this. He said morality comes from pure reason. He said we must follow a universal duty, no matter what we feel.

I disagree. I think Kant's system is not real. It is make-believe.

The Real Foundation

Morality starts with something deeper. It starts with an intuition. An intuition is a knowledge we are born with. We do not learn it from books or parents.

We are born knowing that other beings are like us. This is not just a feeling like hunger or anger. This is a hardwired recognition. It is built into our biology.

We see this in animals too. Animals help each other. They cannot stand to see others suffer. This proves the recognition is real and natural.

What About People Who Lack Empathy?

You might ask: "If this intuition is universal, why are some people not empathetic?"

The answer is simple. Look at mathematics. Humans are born with an intuition for numbers. We can all learn to count. But some people are terrible at math. They struggle with basic sums. Does that mean the math intuition is not there? No. It is there. But it is weak, untrained, or blocked by fear and bad teaching.

Empathy is exactly the same. Some people are terrible at empathy. They are desensitized. Trauma, selfish habits, or bad environments can shut down their empathy. But their intuition is still inside them. It is just sleeping or broken. It can be trained back, just like math can be learned again.

How It Works

We cannot experience others directly. We only experience them through our own mind. We use outside information and our own feelings to understand them. But the recognition itself is still true.

Sometimes people do bad things. They may not feel guilt at that moment. But the intuition is still there. It is just sleeping. It can wake up again if they become more conscious. This is why a bad person is still wrong. They are breaking a rule that is built into their own nature.

Universality Without Kant

My system is universal. It applies to all beings that have this intuition. Humans have it. Many animals have it too. If only rocks and stars existed, there would be no morality. But we exist. So morality is real.

Kant built his system on reason. But reason alone is empty. It does not give us a real foundation. My system is built on something solid: our shared nature.

The Simple Rule

The rule is simple: Do not hurt others. Why? Because others are like us. Hurting them is like hurting ourselves.

Just like math, we all have the basic equipment to understand this. Some of us are better at it than others. But the foundation is there in everyone. We just need to wake up, practice, and see it clearly.
