# How Do You Know?

_Some people say one thing. Others say the opposite. What do you say?_

_Write as much or as little as you want. If a question bores you, just write "boring question" and move on. No pressure._

---

## 1. Truth

Some people think there is no real truth. Everything is just opinion. Right and wrong depend on your culture. Beauty is in the eye of the beholder. Even math and science aren't fixed—if we had evolved differently, we'd have different math and science.

Others believe truth is real. There is a way things are, whether anyone believes it or not. Math is discovered, not invented. Some things are just true, no matter what.

**What do you think?**

---

## 2. Knowing

Some say we can't know anything for sure. Science is always changing. Our senses trick us. Maybe you're just a brain in a jar, or a butterfly dreaming it's a man.

Others say some things are certain. You can't doubt that you're doubting. You know you exist. That's the foundation.

**What do you think? Can you know anything for sure? How do you know you exist? Could you be wrong about that?**

---

## 3. Limits

Some say there are things we can never know. The human mind has limits. We can't understand the world just by thinking—we need experience, data, experiments.

Others say reason can grasp the deepest truths. Philosophy can tell us about reality without a lab. The world is understandable because it's structured like a mind.

**What do you think? Can we understand reality through reason alone? How do you know you're awake right now? What's the difference between a dream and this?**

---

## 4. Trust

Some say trust the experts. Trust science. Trust your gut. Or: trust no one.

Others say you need your own rules. Blind trust is dangerous. Authority is not truth.

**What do you think? How do you decide who to trust? What makes thinking good instead of just smart-sounding? How do your thoughts match the world? Do they?**

---

## 5. Mind and Matter

Some say matter is the most basic thing. The universe is atoms and energy. Mind is just what the brain does—a trick of chemistry.

Others say mind is the most basic thing. Matter is an idea in the mind. Without someone to see it, there is nothing to see.

**What do you think? Is mind more basic? Does matter exist without a mind to see it? A tree falls in an empty forest—does it make a sound? Does the forest even exist?**

---

## 6. Consciousness

Some say consciousness is just brain activity. It's an illusion. Or: it's a mystery we may never solve.

Others say consciousness is the ground of everything. It can't be reduced to matter. It's what makes everything else possible.

**What do you think? What is consciousness? Is it made of something, or is it the thing that makes? How does a brain make a thought? Does it?**

---

## 7. Space and Time

Some say space and time are real, like containers. They exist even if no one is looking.

Others say they're just how we see things. Made up by the mind. Without a mind, there's no distance, no time.

**What do you think? Are space and time real, or just how we see things? Can there be distance without someone to measure it? Can there be time without someone to remember?**

---

## 8. God

Some say God is a man in the sky. Or: God is the universe. Or: there is no God.

Others say God is the ground of all being. Not a being among beings, but what makes anything exist at all. A universal Mind.

**What do you believe? Why? Is your God a mind? A force? A story? Did you choose this belief, or inherit it? Have you ever doubted it? What happened?**

---

## 9. Purpose

Some say life has no built-in purpose—we make our own. Or: there is a divine plan.

Others say purpose is discovered, not invented. It's written into reality. We are here for a reason.

**What do you think? Why are we conscious? Is there a purpose to any of this? Did you find it, or make it? What's the meaning of your life?**

---

## 10. Death

Some say death is the end. Lights out. Or: we go to heaven, hell, reincarnate.

Others say death is a transition. Consciousness continues. What we do here matters forever.

**What do you think? What happens when you die? How do you know? Does it scare you? Should it?**

---

## 11. Good and Evil

Some say good and evil are relative. Or: they are absolute laws written into reality.

Others say morality is a human invention. Or: it's grounded in God's nature.

**What do you think? Are good and evil real, or invented? How do you decide what's right? Why is there suffering? Does it have a point?**

---

## 12. Living

Some say the good life is about happiness, success, family, money.

Others say the good life is about virtue, wisdom, peace of mind.

**What do you think? What's a good life? What do you actually want? Do you have it? What's stopping you?**

---

## 13. Things

Some say you are what you own. More stuff = more happiness. Or: minimalism is the key.

Others say possessions tie you down. They bind you to a world that will let you down. Let go.

**What do you think? Do you own your things, or do they own you? Could you give it all up? Would you be freer if you did?**

---

## 14. Change

Some say change is hard. Resist it. Or: change is the only constant.

Others say everything changes. Nothing lasts. Learn to flow.

**What do you think? How do you deal with change? What do you hold on to? What should you let go of?**

---

## 15. Alone and Together

Some say you need others to be happy. Or: you should be self-sufficient.

Others say community is essential. Or: solitude is where you find yourself.

**What do you think? Do you need other people? What does a good community look like? Could you live off your own land? Would you want to?**

---

## 16. Learning

Some say education is about getting a job. Or: it's about becoming wise.

Others say it's about passing on a way of life. Or: it's about learning to think for yourself.

**What do you think? How do you learn best? What should education actually teach? What would you pass on to a kid? How would you teach it differently than you were taught?**

---

## 17. Yourself

Some say you should live according to your beliefs. Or: beliefs are just opinions.

Others say your life is your argument. What you do matters more than what you say.

**What do you think? Has any of this changed how you live? If you started over, what would you keep? What would you throw away? What are you waiting for?**
