### **PAGE 1**

**PANEL 1**
Close-up on a piece of cheap rice paper. A thick, clumsy calligraphy brush is dragging across it, leaving a horribly warped, shaky character for **"Righteousness" (正 - Zhèng)**. The vertical line looks like a zigzagging lightning bolt.

- **XIUQUAN (V.O.):** Master Kong once said, _"If the mat is not straight, the Master will not sit."_

**PANEL 2**
Pull back. We are inside a cramped, rustic Hakka farmhouse. **JINXIN** (15, sweating profusely, tongue sticking out in concentration) is holding the brush. **HONG XIUQUAN** (7, small, looking like a flawless chibi anime intellectual) is casually leaning over his shoulder, holding a cup of tea with an insufferably serene expression.

- **XIUQUAN:** ...But looking at your stroke work, Brother Jinxin, the Master wouldn't just refuse to sit. He would likely suffer severe vertigo and trip over his own robes. Truly, your brushwork possesses a... revolutionary defiance of geometry.
- **JINXIN (grinning, completely missing the insult):** Ah! You think it's dynamic? Thank you, little brother! I felt a sudden burst of artistic energy in my wrist.

**PANEL 3**
Suddenly, a heavy wooden ruler slams down on the table right next to Xiuquan’s tea! **THE VILLAGE ELDER/TUTOR** (old, bearded, wearing a slightly frayed scholar's cap) is towering over them, steam practically blowing out of his ears.

- **ELDER:** Impudent child! How dare you use the words of the sacred Sages to mock your elder brother’s diligent efforts?! You speak of geometry, yet you lack basic filial piety!

**PANEL 4**
The Elder snatches the practice paper away from Jinxin, raising his hand to clip Xiuquan over the ear. Xiuquan doesn't even flinch; he just sips his tea, eyes tracking the paper.

- **ELDER:** To correct another, one must first possess perfect understanding! Your brother is writing from the classic _Analects_, and his—

**PANEL 5**
Extreme close-up on the Elder's eyes. They go wide. The anger instantly evaporates into sheer, jaw-dropping shock. In the background, Xiuquan points a tiny, polite finger at the corner of the page.

- **XIUQUAN (calmly):** If you examine the lower radical, Teacher, Jinxin inadvertently wrote the archaic Han-dynasty variant for 'crooked' instead of 'straight.' By pointing out his architectural failure, I actually saved him from committing accidental heresy against the Emperor's preferred script.

**PANEL 6**
The Elder stares at the paper, then at the 7-year-old. He drops his ruler. He is sweating now, realizing the kid is 100% correct, having memorized texts the Elder hasn't looked at in twenty years.

- **ELDER (stuttering, inner monologue):** _By the ancestors... the boy didn't just spot a bad stroke. He cross-referenced a centuries-old bureaucratic script variation. He's... he's a freak._

### **PAGE 2**

**PANEL 1**
A montage sequence. A grumpy old farmer hands over a single, dented copper coin. A tearful grandmother offers a very confused chicken. Jinxin and Decong are handing over their favorite fishing rods. All of it is going into a wooden chest labeled: _“THE HONG XIUQUAN IMPERIAL EXAM FUND.”_

- **CAPTION (NARRATOR):** It takes a village to raise a child. It takes an entire district's life savings to fund an insufferably smug prodigy.

**PANEL 2**
Xiuquan, now a young teenager, is sitting in a pristine, newly built study room paid for by the village. He is casually tossing peanuts into his mouth with one hand while effortlessly writing two different essays simultaneously using brushes in both his left and right hands.

- **XIUQUAN (thinking):** _They want me to become a Grand Secretary so I can exempt the village from taxes. A modest goal. Personally, I’m just looking forward to having a desk where nobody brings manure within a three-mile radius._

**PANEL 3**
Xiuquan stands at the village boundary, packed and ready. The entire town is cheering, weeping, holding up massive banners that say _"FUTURE FIRST-PLACE SCHOLAR HONG!"_ He waves a hand lazily over his shoulder, entirely unbothered.

- **XIUQUAN:** Relax, everyone. The Imperial Civil Service is just a giant machine of memorization. And my brain happens to be a perfect recording device. This is going to be a piece of cake.

### **PAGE 3**

**PANEL 1**
An epic, cinematic view of Guangzhou. The city is sprawling, crowded, and intimidating. But in the foreground, young adult Xiuquan is walking through the city gates wearing a ridiculously flashy, bright red scholar's robe. He is fanning himself lazily, looking like a total rockstar.

- **XIUQUAN (thinking):** _Look at these anxious, sweating fools. Memorizing texts? Trembling? Please. This exam isn't a test; it’s a coronation. I’ll be back in the village by Tuesday, eating victory chicken._

**PANEL 2**
Inside the infamous examination compound. It looks like a dystopian, Kafkaesque nightmare—rows upon rows of thousands of tiny, isolated wooden shacks stretching into infinity. Guard towers loom overhead.

- **CAPTION (NARRATOR):** The Imperial Civil Service Exam. A brutal, multi-day ordeal of sleep deprivation, rigid formatting, and psychological warfare.

**PANEL 3**
Inside Xiuquan’s tiny cell. While other candidates are weeping, losing their minds, and writing with trembling hands, Xiuquan is lounging backward, balancing his stool on two legs. He is effortlessly flying through his paper, yawning.

- **XIUQUAN (muttering):** _Question four... 'Analyze the cosmic harmony of the tax code.' Yawn. Did a toddler write these prompts? Absolute cake._

**PANEL 4**
Smash-cut to: **RESULTS DAY.**
A massive, chaotic crowd of thousands of men is crammed in front of the "Dragon Board"—a giant wall where the names of the passing candidates are posted. People are fainting, screaming with joy, or tearing their clothes in despair.

**PANEL 5**
Xiuquan is standing right at the front, arms crossed, tapping his foot. He looks at the top of the list, expecting his name to be carved in gold. He looks down a bit. Then further down.

**PANEL 6**
Close-up on Xiuquan’s face. The smugness has completely drained. His eyes are hollow, blank white circles. The camera pans over the list. His name isn't at the top. It’s not in the middle. It’s not even at the absolute dead bottom.

- **XIUQUAN (vibrating with shock):** _...There is no cake. There never was any cake. The machine... didn't even look at me._

**PANEL 3**
Back in Guanlubu Village. Xiuquan is sitting on the edge of the old well, staring down into the dark water. The colorful, expensive scholar's robes are gone, replaced by standard, faded peasant clothes. He looks completely deflated.

- **XIUQUAN (thinking):** _What if the math didn't fail? What if... I failed?_

**PANEL 4**
Xiuquan grips his head, his face twisting into a hyper-dramatic anime expression of existential dread. Spirals in his eyes, background turning into a void of dark ink.

- **XIUQUAN (thinking):** _Was I actually a genius? Or was I just a big fish in a microscopic pond? Am I only "smart" compared to Jinxin and a water buffalo?! How am I supposed to know?!_

**PANEL 5**
He stands up, shouting at the empty sky, tears streaming down his face in comedic, exaggerated torrents.

- **XIUQUAN:** Why is this world so utterly, systematically unfair?! I memorized fifty thousand lines of syntax! I analyzed the economic infrastructure of the Han Dynasty! Why won't a single, faceless bureaucrat just open their eyes and give me a single damn chance?!

- **CAPTION (NARRATOR):** When the soul is crushed by a weight too heavy for the waking world to bear... it looks for an exit.

Close-up on Xiuquan’s face, eyes closed. A soft, glowing, iridescent vapor begins to float upward from his forehead. It’s his spirit, looking peaceful for the first time in years, drifting away from his physical body.

- **CAPTION (NARRATOR):** To survive the absolute annihilation of his hopes, Xiuquan’s mind triggers the ultimate evolutionary defense mechanism: The Cosmic Escape Room.

**PANEL 3**
A massive, breathtaking splash page. The dark, cramped room completely shatters like glass, revealing a magnificent, sparkling celestial realm. It looks like a high-budget, magical fantasy—clouds made of literal gold dust, waterfalls of liquid starlight, and sweeping jade palaces stretching into infinity.

**PANEL 4**
Xiuquan is floating in mid-air, bathed in warm, gentle light. His tattered peasant clothes magically transform into a flawless, glowing robe woven from literal sunbeams. His posture relaxes completely.

- **XIUQUAN (gasping, crying tears of pure relief):** _It doesn't hurt anymore. The noise... the exams... they're gone. It’s so warm here._

**PANEL 5**
An impossibly massive, golden, glowing figure (The Heavenly Father) appears in the sky. He looks like a giant, fiercely loving anime dad with a majestic beard, radiating pure, comforting energy. He reaches down with a hand the size of a mountain, gently cradling Xiuquan.

- **THE HEAVENLY FATHER:** Welcome home, my son. Do not weep for the dirt of the mortal world.
- **PANEL INSERT:** A giant, golden celestial stamp slams down onto a cosmic scroll, completely obliterating a tiny, pathetic caricature of the Qing Dynasty Emperor.
- **THE HEAVENLY FATHER:** Those bureaucratic fools didn't reject you because you failed. They rejected you because _you are far too grand for their tiny, broken machine._

### **PAGE 6**

**PANEL 1**
Inside the dusty family home. **JINXIN** and **DECONG** are standing over Xiuquan’s bed, looking worried. Jinxin is holding a damp washcloth. Suddenly, Xiuquan’s eyes snap open. They are literally glowing with a blinding, sparkling, shounen-anime protagonist energy. He sits straight up, throwing the blankets off like a cape.

- **JINXIN (yelping):** Ah! He’s alive! Quick, get the rice water!
- **XIUQUAN (booming voice, perfectly composed):** Silence, mortals. The rice water can wait. I have just returned from a board meeting with the Almighty, and I have discovered my true calling.

**PANEL 2**
Xiuquan steps outside into the sunlight. He catches his reflection in a water bucket. He still looks like a regular guy, but he strikes a hyper-dramatic, confident pose.

- **XIUQUAN (inner monologue):** _The scholars saw me as a peasant. The peasants saw me as a scholar. I was a glitch in their system. But that glitch... is my superpower. I speak the language of the elite, but I bleed the mud of the fields. I am the bridge._

**PANEL 3**
A local village market square. Xiuquan climbs onto an empty wooden crate. He has the total, relaxed confidence of a rockstar sound-checking a microphone. A few confused farmers, holding cabbages and ducks, stop to look.

- **XIUQUAN (projecting flawlessly):** My brothers! For centuries, the high-and-mighty bureaucrats in the capital have quoted the Sages to explain why _you_ must starve so _they_ can wear silk! They tell you it is the "Will of Heaven" that your taxes double while your bellies empty!

**PANEL 4**
Xiuquan points dramatically at a fancy, distant government carriage rolling past. His eyes narrow with brilliant, comedic intensity.

- **XIUQUAN:** But I have read their sacred books! And more importantly, I have spoken directly to the Landlord of the Universe! And let me tell you—those tax-collecting demons are completely misinterpreting the lease agreement! Heaven doesn't want your silver; Heaven wants you to stop bowing to corrupt middlemen!

**PANEL 5**
The crowd’s reaction. It’s an absolute comedic brain-melt. Their eyes are wide, jaws dropped to the floor, little stylized hearts and stars exploding around their heads.

- **OLD FARMER (tears streaming):** He... he speaks like a god, but he smells like fertilizer, just like us!
- **YOUNG PEASANT:** I don't even know what half those big words mean, but I am 100% ready to run through a brick wall for this man!

### **PAGE 7**

**PANEL 1**
A few months later. A gorgeous, cinematic shot of a misty mountain pathway in Guangxi. Walking down the path is Xiuquan, looking like an absolute anime icon of revolutionary charisma. Walking a half-step behind him is his childhood friend, **FENG YUNSHAN**, who looks like the ultimate, hyper-enthusiastic, note-taking sidekick.

- **YUNSHAN (scribbling furiously on a scroll):** Teach them... the Sages were... middle managers... Got it! Brother Hong, your sermon yesterday about the "Celestial Tenancy Agreement" completely broke the blacksmith's brain. He threw his idols in the river and joined us on the spot!
- **XIUQUAN (fanning himself, supreme confidence restored):** Of course he did, Yunshan. The bureaucracy spoke to them in tax codes; I speak to them in liberation.

**PANEL 2**
A massive open-air gathering in a valley. Hundreds of Hakka peasants, miners, and laborers are packed together, listening intently. Xiuquan stands on a natural rock platform.

- **XIUQUAN:** The magistrates tell you that you suffer because of bad karma. I tell you that you suffer because a bunch of corrupt silver-hoarders in Beijing are blocking your direct connection to the Almighty! You don’t need an Imperial degree to be righteous. You just need to realize who actually runs this world.

**PANEL 3**
The crowd goes absolutely wild. It’s a beautifully stylized anime crowd shot—people throwing their farming hats into the air, tough mountain miners weeping tears of joy, and a line of new converts eagerly signing their names into Xiuquan's new ledger.

- **CROWD:** Long live the Heavenly Younger Brother! Down with the middle managers!

**PANEL 4**
A massive, epic split-screen panel demonstrating the ultimate irony of fate.

- **LEFT SIDE OF PANEL:** A close-up of a lazy, overweight Qing Dynasty local magistrate back in the city. He is yawning, stamping a stack of tax documents without reading them, and tossing a tiny, minor report about "a local peasant street-preacher" straight into a roaring fireplace.
- **RIGHT SIDE OF PANEL:** Xiuquan standing on the mountain peak, looking out over the vast, sprawling map of China. His eyes are sharp, brilliant, and completely terrifying. A tiny, smug smile plays on his lips.
- **CAPTION (NARRATOR):** The Imperial Examination Board rejected Hong Xiuquan because they didn't have the time, the care, or the competence to look at a single piece of paper.

**PANEL 5**
A grand, sweeping silhouette shot of Xiuquan and his rapidly growing army of disciples marching forward into the sunset, carrying banners of the God Worshipping Society. The shadows they cast look massive, stretching all the way across the landscape of the empire.

- **CAPTION (NARRATOR):** To save themselves a minor administrative hassle, the bureaucracy gave a genius boy nothing left to lose.
- **CAPTION (NARRATOR):** And in return... he is going to give them a revolution.
