We often think real martial arts are about fists. They are about hitting and hurting. But I have changed my mind.

Physical fighting is actually the least effective martial art.

The real weapons are not obvious. Especially today.

In the modern world, knowledge is your best defense.

Learn how the law works. You can make anyone behave well. Even if they threaten you. Just explain what trouble they could get into.

But what if they do not understand? Then outwit them. Show them they are socially inferior. You win.

You can also use arguments. Emotional arguments. Logical arguments. You win again.

What if physical fighting is unavoidable? Your knowledge still saves you. Look at your environment. Throw things. Run away. Shout for help. There are many ways to avoid harm.

True martial arts are about avoiding physical contact. This is almost always possible.

What if your opponent is an animal? Physical martial arts may not save you. But knowledge might. Learn the animal's traits. Outsmart it. It will be hard. Sometimes impossible. But still better than fighting.

So here is the truth. Physical martial arts are mostly for show today. They are not useless for fitness or discipline. But for real damage? For real protection?

The real martial arts are knowledge. Knowledge of law. Knowledge of persuasion. Knowledge of how people work.

These are the things that truly win.
