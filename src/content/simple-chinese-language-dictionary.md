**Legend**

- `[R]` root — one of the 171 generative content roots
- `[F]` function morpheme — closed grammar/function set
- `[C]` compound — 的-phrase or root compound built from roots/function morphemes
- `[L]` loan — borrowed from standard Mandarin; not derived from roots

Format: **word** `[class]` = 简单话 _(literal gloss for [C] entries)_
